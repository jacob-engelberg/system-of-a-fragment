System of a Fragment: Interactive Learning Resource

This is an interactive learning resource designed to aid readers of Raymond Bellour’s “System of a Fragment: The Birds.” Bellour’s text was first published as “‘Les Oiseaux’ : analyse d’une séquence,” Cahiers du cinéma 216 (1969): 24-39. It was later amended, translated, and republished as “System of a Fragment: The Birds,” translated by Ben Brewster, in The Analysis of Film, edited by Constance Penley (Indiana University Press, 2001). The resource has been designed with the 2001 text as its reference.

The film clips presented here are used solely for educational purposes and in accordance with the educational exception under Article 16 of the Dutch Copyright Act (Auteurswet). All copyright and related rights remain with their respective rights holders.
